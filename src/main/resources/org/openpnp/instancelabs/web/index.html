Surfaces

Probed heights for the board (every dot takes its Z from this plane) and the blank (purge row, dot ladder and touch-off). Touch at least 3 points spread around the area with N1 (Z in OpenPnP's frame), or paste points, e.g. [[x, y, z], …] or the points of a zmap.json. Saving the board surface while a board is planned ties it to that board's position: probe again after any re-seat (one seating was tilted 0.9 mm), then plan again.

Purge row

Each spot is used once (I9). When the row is full, wipe it and press reset. The number of purge dots per board is in the dispense profile.

    Dispense profile

    The recipe of every dot on a board and on the purge row (bench recipe 2026-10-28: 18 psi, half a screw turn, 250 ms dwell). Changing it makes existing plans stale: plan the board again.

    Pressure: set by hand on the regulator; narrow window: 16 psi starved the screw, 20 psi drooled. Dwell: the dot-size knob.

    Faster than the bench's 180 rpm: never run the screw faster than the paste feeds it (it pulls in air and needs 30 to 50 turns to recover).

      Screw start angle

      The screw angle is counted from the B motor steps since the controller board was powered on. Switching the controller off restarts that count, so the angle must be measured again; after a reconnect without a power cycle press "Still valid".
