Board drawings for the browser tests (devproxy.py --fixtures), generated by
BoardArtFixturesTest (src/test/java/org/openpnp/instancelabs/board) from the
KiCad fixtures of src/test/resources/kicad. board-map-generated.json names the
file of each drawing; the files are named by their art id, so they change when
the drawing changes. Sources, commits, changes and licences of the designs:
src/test/resources/kicad/LICENSE.txt.

Drawings of ftp.kicad_pcb (ftp.board.xml), of ftp.board.xml itself
(ftp-fallback.board.xml) and of mobo.kicad_pcb (mobo.board.xml):
derived from the stripped copies of the Opulo designs, which are available
under the CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 - Weakly Reciprocal
(CERN-OHL-W v2): https://cern.ch/cern-ohl. The projects' notice:

    LumenPnP is (c) by Opulo

The projects' logo, branding and marks are reserved; the copies they are drawn
from have them removed, and BoardArtFixturesTest checks that no drawing names
the project or the company.
